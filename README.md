# Aloka Green County — Luxury Villa Plots

> Premium BMRDA Approved Residential Villa Plots located in Arsanahalli, close to STRR (Near Whitefield Exit), East Bengaluru.

## Overview
Aloka Green County is a luxury gated community villa plot project crafted for tranquil modern living and high-growth capital appreciation.

- **Location**: Arsanahalli, Close to STRR & Near STRR-Whitefield Exit, Devanagonthi, East Bengaluru
- **Approvals**: BMRDA & MPA Approved, Clear Titles, Bank Loan Approved
- **Plot Sizes**: 30×40 (1,200 sq.ft), 30×50 (1,500 sq.ft), 40×60 (2,400 sq.ft)
- **Amenities**: Grand Clubhouse, Swimming Pool, Landscaped Gardens, 40ft & 30ft Blacktop Roads, 24/7 CCTV & Security, Underground Electricity & Drainage
